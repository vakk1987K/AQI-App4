## 1. Introduction & Overview

**AQI-App: Air Quality & Weather** ("we", "our", or "us") provides live ambient Air Quality Index (AQI) tracking, particulate matter monitoring (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, CO, SO₂, Ozone), temperature metrics, rain arrival countdowns, and health advisories.

This Privacy Policy informs you about our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our application, ensuring strict compliance with the **Google Play Developer Program Policies**, **Google Play User Data Policies**, and international privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA).

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### A. Location Information (Coarse and Precise Geolocation)
- **Purpose:** If you grant location permissions (via the "Locate Me" button or automatic station locator), the app requests your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) strictly to identify the nearest ambient air quality monitoring station and provide local meteorological forecasts and rain alerts.
- **Ephemeral Processing Only:** Your location coordinates are transmitted directly and securely to public meteorological API endpoints in real-time. **We NEVER store, record, log, or persist your location on remote servers or databases.** Once the ambient data is loaded into your browser session, your precise coordinates are discarded.
- **Optional Use:** Providing location data is completely optional. You can manually type or select any city (such as Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Delhi, Mumbai, Vijayawada, or London) to view full AQI reports without ever granting location access.

### B. Personal Data (Zero Collection)
- The app **does NOT** require account creation, login, or registration.
- We **do NOT** collect, store, or solicit names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device identifiers (IMEI/Android ID), physical addresses, financial details, or biometric data.

### C. Device & Diagnostics
- Standard non-identifying technical data (e.g. browser type, operating system version) is handled locally by the client device to render the user interface and responsive views.

## 3. Third-Party Data Services & Transmission Security

To provide accurate atmospheric, pollutant, and rain forecast readings, the app queries:
- **Open-Meteo API** (Meteorological and Air Quality models): [https://open-meteo.com/](https://open-meteo.com/)
- **EPA AirNow / WHO Breakpoints**: Mathematical index standards published for public public health.

All data transmission between your device and external data providers is encrypted end-to-end using **HTTPS and Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3)**.

## 7. User Rights and Controls

- **Revoking Location Access:** You can enable or revoke device location permissions at any time via your Android device settings:  
  *Android Settings → Apps → AQI-App → Permissions → Location → Deny / Don't allow*.
- **Notification Controls:** Push and rain alert notifications can be toggled on/off in the app settings or via Android system notification settings.
